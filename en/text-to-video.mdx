---
title: "Text to video"
description: "Use the online studio or asynchronous API"
---

In the signed-in video studio, choose a key, model, ratio, resolution, and duration, then submit a prompt. Video jobs are asynchronous: keep the task ID and poll its status until a result or error is returned.

The public video task endpoint is `https://aibal.ai/api/v3/contents/generations/tasks`. Use the model and fields currently accepted by the console.
