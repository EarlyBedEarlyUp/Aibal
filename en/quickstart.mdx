For your first visit, follow this sequence, choose the group and models available to your account, and complete a generation in the web app.

## Complete your first experience

<Card title="1. Register an account" href="/en/register">
  Create your aibal.ai account and sign in.
</Card>

<Card title="2. Top up your account" href="/en/recharge">
  Learn the USDT and redemption-code methods and confirm the credited amount.
</Card>

<Card title="3. Create an API Key" href="/en/create-api-key">
  Create a dedicated Key and check its group, quota and expiration.
</Card>

<Card title="4. Try the web app" href="/en/web-quickstart">
  Generate an image, download it and review usage.
</Card>

<Note>
Web and client requests may incur charges. Check current console models and prices. If a request's outcome is uncertain, review usage before retrying.
</Note>
