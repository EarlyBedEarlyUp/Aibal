---
title: "Recharge: USDT and redeem codes"
description: "Add usable balance to your account"
---

Use the signed-in recharge page to select an available USDT network and amount. Copy the address from the current order, confirm the network, and keep the transaction hash until the order is credited.

For a redeem code, open **Redeem code**, enter the complete code, and submit it once. Network, rate, expiry, and code rules are defined by the current console.
