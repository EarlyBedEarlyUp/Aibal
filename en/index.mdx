aibal.ai gives your applications one consistent gateway to AI services. Create a key once, choose the model you need, and keep integrations simple as your team grows.

<Tip>
  Start with the [quickstart](/en/quickstart) to send your first request in a few minutes.
</Tip>

## What you can do

- Connect applications through a familiar OpenAI-compatible API.
- Organize keys, teams, and model access from one dashboard.
- Route requests to available providers while keeping your application configuration stable.
- Review usage and spending so every project stays predictable.
- Give each environment its own key and limits.

## Who it is for

aibal.ai is designed for product teams, developers, agencies, and independent builders who need a clear way to manage several AI-powered applications. Use it for prototypes, internal tools, customer-facing products, and shared team workspaces.

## A predictable integration layer

Your application talks to one documented endpoint and receives a consistent response format. You can change an upstream model or provider in the dashboard without rewriting every client. This keeps experiments fast and production changes deliberate.
