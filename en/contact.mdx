You can contact us using either option below. You do not need to sign in to aibal.ai first.

## Telegram group

Open the [AIbal.ai Support group](https://t.me/aibal_support) and follow the prompts in Telegram to join.

## Email

- [hi@aibal.ai](mailto:hi@aibal.ai)
- [support@aibal.ai](mailto:support@aibal.ai)

Click an email address to open your email app. If it does not open, copy the address and compose a message in your usual email service.

## Describe your question

Briefly describe what you were doing, what happened, and when it happened. Before sharing screenshots, hide personal details, API Keys, and payment information. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

You can also check the [user guide](/en/platform-overview) for instructions related to your task.
