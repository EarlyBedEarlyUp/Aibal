## Check requests and charges

Open console [usage records](https://aibal.ai/usage) and compare the time, model and Key with your requests. If usage looks unexpected, disable the relevant Key, keep request IDs, task IDs and redacted errors, and contact support. A network timeout does not prove that no task or charge was created; do not retry blindly.

<Note>
Keep passwords, verification codes, API Keys and full payment details private. Disable and replace an exposed Key. Redact logs and screenshots before sharing.
</Note>
